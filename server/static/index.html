HOW PARKTRACK 360 WORKS

A fully automated, sensor-backed parking experience designed to eliminate parking stress.

01

Automated Arrival

Drive up to the entry boom barrier. The overhead camera reads your vehicle tag, verifies vacancy, and lifts the gate seamlessly.

02

Live Dynamic Guidance

Follow the digital LED matrix and LCD display to locate your assigned bay across Ground or First Floor.

03

Ultrasonic In-Slot Sensor

Pull into the bay. The calibrated ultrasonic sensor confirms your parking, switches the overhead LED from Green to Red, and starts your session.

04

Express UPI Checkout

When ready to depart, open our payment page on your phone, scan the dynamic UPI QR code, and exit effortlessly.
